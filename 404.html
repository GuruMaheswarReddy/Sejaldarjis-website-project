About me

I, Ms. Sejal Darji (Mrs. Adi-Das), a happy-go-lucky, positive and family oriented person who enjoys connecting with children, making learning enjoyable.

I believe teaching is not only about the syllabus, but a value addition to understanding a child, recognising their strength and potential. Helping them become confident learners and better humans.

I aim to create an environment where students feel comfortable expressing themselves.

We nurture talent, confidence, enhance communication and develop independent thinking. Encouraging a strong positive attitude towards situations, people and life.

With my background in HR, leadership, change management and earning "English teaching professional certificates", I bring subject knowledge and people’s skills; facilitating a learner-centric approach.
